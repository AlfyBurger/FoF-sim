## Rulings from the official Errata & Clarifications (10 May 2025)
Errata relevant to the Normandy scope:
- E1 8.6.3: third point numbered 3 (typo only).
- E2 8.11: Out of Ammo **fire teams** become Unspotted if they Fall Back out of LOS.
- E3 9.0 example: at Night +1, a −3 Grenade VOF is stronger than a −3 Heavy Weapons VOF because Grenade VOF ignores light modifiers.
- E4 Normandy (and Naktong) Experience table, Offensive, last row: "Clear another card that had a B or C PC marker" (a card other than an Objective or Attack Position card).
- (E5–E13 concern Naktong, Heartbreak Ridge, Vietnam: recorded in the source file, applied when those campaigns come into scope.)
Clarifications:
- C1 4.2: no minimum draw for any attempt except Spotting; if experience modifiers reduce the draw to 0 cards, the action is not possible (typical for Call for Fire, Seek Cover).
- C2 4.2.2/4.2.4 and Action Menu footnote: action "may be available to Pinned or LAT units with some restrictions (see 4.2.5)" — not always available to Pinned units.
- C3 6.5.2: all Reconstitute actions need an HQ or Staff to give the order, even under General Initiative (see 4.1.3).
- C4 7.10.4 & 6.3.6: a Grenade Miss modifier used as the actual VOF (no other VOF on the card) in Point Blank combat affects all units on the card.
- C5 8.4.3: the restriction concerns actual enemy units in a package only; other package markers (VOF, illumination…) may go on cards with existing enemy units.
- C6 8.12/10.5.1: an activated AT-capable enemy infantry unit uses AT Fire (10.5.2A) in the AT Combat Phase if possible.
- C8 10.8.5/10.8.7: attack helicopters and fixed-wing gunships affect all units on a card, even at point blank.
- C9: village card cover (Vietnam, Naktong) works as in Normandy/Keep up the Fire: a package noted "out of cover" does not draw for building cover.
- (C7, C10: Vietnam only.)

## Rulings settled with the user
- U1 Action deck (Q1): the user's deck has 51 cards = 50 Action cards + Reshuffle card. FM1 p.7 confirms the box deck is 50 + Reshuffle + 4 hint cards, the hint cards being removed before play (so "55 cards" in product listings includes the hints).
- U2 Learning path: follow Field Manual 1 step by step, with rulebook references at each step.
- U3 No Contact bonus (was Q2): apply rule 4.1.2C (+1 to HQ Command draws while the Activity Level is No Contact) in every exercise, including FM1 PAC Runs 1–2 where the walkthrough leaves it out. (User, 2026-11-16.)

## Settled by the rulebook (cross-check)
- R1 Card words: **Cover** = success for Seek Cover (4.2.2e; example p.19), **Rally** = success for Rally (6.5, "look for the word Rally"). The 2.8.2 legend only groups them.
- R2 Hit effect letters (6.4.3): C Casualty, P Paralyzed Team, L Litter Team, F Fire Team, A Assault Team; first letter to the 1st step hit, second to the 2nd; a 1-step unit uses the first letter only.
- R3 Always draw the full number of cards even if success shows early; the reshuffle card is not counted, finish the draw, then reshuffle everything (2.2.3).
